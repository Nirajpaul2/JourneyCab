## Interpretation

- Shared origins (Siwan/Maharajganj and Rasulpur/Chainpur) are separate selectable routes with the same printed price.
- All prices apply only in the printed direction. No implicit reverse fare is generated.
- The banner specifies a five-seater AC vehicle. The booking UI conservatively allows four passengers plus a driver and applies these fares to hatchback/sedan categories. Actual model, luggage and capacity need team confirmation.
- Toll tax and parking are payable separately. Tax treatment is unspecified; no invented 5% sample tax is added. The team must agree final charges and inclusions.
- The origin printed as बड़हिया is transcribed as Barhiya. Pickup address and locality must be checked by staff, especially for ambiguous names such as Chainpur and Rasulpur.
- Larger cars, round trips and local rentals have no supplied prices and are unavailable for online booking until separately priced.
- No distances or durations are invented or displayed. For allocation conflict checks, new requests conservatively occupy 24 hours from departure. Staff must verify actual travel and repositioning needs.
- Existing stored requests and approved fares are not rewritten.
