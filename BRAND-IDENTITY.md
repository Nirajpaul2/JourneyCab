## Image generation prompt

Design a refined professional emblem for Aryan Tour and Travel, an Indian cab company. The attached image is visual reference only: retain the ideas of a blue sedan, red circular arc, and a small mountain/road cue, but redraw as an original premium, minimal flat brand icon with precise confident curves. Square composition, icon occupies about 90% of canvas. A modern navy blue sedan in elegant three-quarter side view with white negative-space windows, a restrained crimson red open circular arc suggesting a journey, and tiny simplified navy mountain peaks behind it. Only navy #102C59, crimson #DF2638, and white negative space. No yellow, no sky or scenery, no airplane, no photographic effects, no shadows, no gradients, no mockup. No letters, words, numbers or tagline at all; website will typeset these separately. Strong silhouette, generous clear negative space, readable at 56px. Genuine transparent background outside emblem and in open areas. Output a single clean logo emblem, not multiple options.
